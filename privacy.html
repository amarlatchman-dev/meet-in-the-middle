Privacy policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Halfwise is built to work without knowing who you are. There are no accounts, and the locations you enter are processed in your browser.

What stays on your device

The people, locations, travel settings and meeting time you enter are saved in your browser's local storage so they're still there next time. Unless you use a group plan (below), they aren't sent to a Halfwise server. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Services your browser talks to

To work, the site loads data from these services. Each receives the request your browser makes (including your IP address) under its own privacy policy:

  • Map tiles and pre-built road data: GitHub Pages and Cloudflare host the site and its map files. Esri provides the background map images.
  • Address search and place names: Photon by Komoot (and OpenStreetMap Nominatim as a backup) receives what you type in the search box, and the coordinates of pins and meeting spots so they can be named.
  • Travel times outside the pre-built areas: the OpenStreetMap routing service (OSRM) at routing.openstreetmap.de and router.project-osrm.org receives the coordinates of your group and candidate meeting spots.
  • Nearby places outside the pre-built areas: Overpass API servers receive the coordinates of the meeting spot.
  • Fonts: Google Fonts.

Your location

If you tap Use my location, your browser asks for permission first. The position is used to add you to the group and isn't stored anywhere except your own browser.

Group plans

If you start or join a group plan, Halfwise stores that plan on Cloudflare (our hosting provider) so everyone in the group can see it: each person's name, their location rounded to about 10 metres, the plan's travel settings and meeting time, votes, and the chosen place. Anyone with the plan link can see this, so only share it with your group. Plans are deleted automatically 7 days after they're created. You can leave a plan at any time, which removes your name, location and vote from it.

To stop abuse, the service counts requests per IP address for a few minutes. IP addresses aren't stored with plans.

Invite links

An invite link contains the group's names, locations (rounded to about 10 metres), travel mode and meeting time, encoded in the link itself. Anyone who has the link can read them. Only share it with people you'd tell where you're coming from.

Ads and analytics

Halfwise may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised ads at Google Ad Settings and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in regions that require it are asked for consent first.

Halfwise uses two cookie-free analytics tools: Cloudflare Web Analytics, for visit counts and page speed, and GoatCounter, for visit counts and which features get used (for example, how often someone taps Find the spot or Invite friends, the travel mode chosen and the number of people in a group). Neither one uses cookies, tracks you across sites, or receives your names, addresses or locations. Like any website, they receive your browser's request, including your IP address, which GoatCounter doesn't store.

Children

Halfwise isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. Questions? Contact us.
