Open Halfwise

Find the fair place to meet

Halfwise picks a meeting spot that's fair for everyone, using real travel times instead of a straight line on a map. Then it shows places to eat, things to do, and free spots nearby.

How it works

  1. Add everyone. Up to 10 people. Search an address, drop a pin, or use your current location. Or send the group an invite link so each person adds themselves.
  2. Pick how you're travelling. Car, transit, bike or walking, and when you're meeting.
  3. Watch the floods. Each person's location floods outward along the real streets, first one by one and then all at once. Where they meet is where it's fair to meet.
  4. Choose your spot. Halfwise shows three options and how they compare, plus a route and a leave-at time for each person so you all arrive together.
  5. Find something to do. Restaurants by cuisine, cafés, bars, activities, parks and free public spaces near the spot, filtered to what's open when you meet.

Three ways to be fair

ChoiceWhat it doesGood for
FairestMakes the longest trip as short as possible.Nobody wants to be the one driving an hour.
BalancedA weighted average of everyone's travel time, where long trips count extra.Most groups. Leans toward where most people are without stranding anyone.
Least totalKeeps everyone's combined travel as low as possible.Saving the most time overall, even if one person travels more.

Where the data comes from

Roads, transit lines and places come from OpenStreetMap, the free map built by volunteers. Halfwise pre-builds the road network for major North American regions so results appear in a second or two. Outside those regions it asks the free OpenStreetMap routing service (OSRM) for travel times.

Travel times are estimates. Driving times don't include live traffic. Transit estimates use subway, LRT, streetcar and commuter rail lines with average waits; buses and exact timetables aren't included yet.
